AirCanvas Gesture Guide

☝️
Draw Mode (Index Up, Middle Down)

Raise your index finger alone. Landmark 8 tracks the tip, drawing smooth ink onto the canvas.

✌️
Hover / Move Mode (Index & Middle Up)

Raise both index and middle fingers together (peace sign). The cursor moves without drawing, so you can reposition your hand.

✋
Pause / Disengage (Open Palm or Fist)

Show all 5 fingers or close into a fist to stop drawing and rest your arm.
